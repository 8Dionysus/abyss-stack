# Governance Storage Plan

Status: seed v0.8.0  
Repo landing: `abyss-stack`  
Layer: Experience Polis Governance

## Purpose

Runtime storage plan for governance cases and decision logs.

v0.8 adds a polis-governance contour around the experience mechanic. Earlier seeds forged capture, distillation, certification, deployment, federation and adoption. This seed asks a harder question: **who may decide when those paths conflict?**

## Local law

- Local owner keeps meaning authority.
- Governance packets must cite evidence and decision refs.
- No direct ToS write or hidden assistant self-change.

## Minimal flow

1. Receive governance route signal.
2. Validate authority and local owner consent.
3. Apply, hold, reject or escalate according to owner law.
4. Emit audit receipt.

## Owner notes

- abyss-stack receives only bounded governance surfaces.
- Policy precedent may inform local decisions but not force local adoption.
- Role doctrine and interpretive authority live in [Agents-of-Abyss](https://github.com/8Dionysus/Agents-of-Abyss/blob/main/docs/EXPERIENCE_WAVE4_POLIS_CONSTITUTION.md) and [Tree-of-Sophia](https://github.com/8Dionysus/Tree-of-Sophia/blob/main/docs/NO_DIRECT_POLIS_GOVERNANCE_WRITE.md); this storage plan only defines persistence and audit behavior.

## Runtime contract

- Store governance cases and decision logs with evidence and decision refs.
- Require owner-local authority and consent before persistence.
- Preserve expiry, audit, and retrieval data without reauthoring doctrine.
- Keep adoption and meaning decisions in the owner layers.
